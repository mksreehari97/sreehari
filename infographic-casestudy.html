OVERVIEW

Kawartha Cottage Country is a stunning, nature-rich region, but regional marketing boards faced a unique challenge: they needed a fresh, dynamic way to appeal to a younger demographic. The goal was to attract millennials to visit, explore, and drive regional tourism.

To bridge this generational communication gap, an infographic featurette was conceptualized. This short, high-energy video was designed to highlight the scenic beauty, recreational activities, and lifestyle attractions of the region, transforming geographical data into a compelling visual hook for weekend getaways and vacations.

OUTCOME & IMPACT

The final featurette successfully transformed regional statistics and highlights into a highly consumable, visually exciting piece of marketing collateral. It serves as a powerful top-of-funnel asset, effectively generating interest and shifting the perspective of millennial audiences toward the Kawartha Cottage Country as a must-visit travel and tourism destination.

THE APPROACH

Millennials consume information rapidly, meaning the featurette had to be concise, visually striking, and immediately valuable. I opted for a clean, modern motion aesthetic. Rather than overwhelming the viewer with numbers, the animation introduces statistics sequentially through slick, easing transitions.

By relying heavily on kinetic typography and iconography, I ensured that the video could communicate its core message effectively even if viewed on social media platforms without sound.

OUTCOME & IMPACT

The final featurette successfully transformed dry regional statistics into a highly consumable, visually exciting piece of marketing collateral. It serves as a powerful top-of-funnel asset, effectively generating interest and shifting the perspective of millennial audiences toward the Kawartha Cottage Country as a viable, attractive place to settle.
